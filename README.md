VOIDFALL is a neon arena-survival roguelite shooter. You pilot a lone ship in a violet void arena: WASD to move, mouse to aim, hold click or Space to fire, Shift to dash through danger with brief invulnerability, and E to drop a nova bomb. Waves of Drifters, Spitters, Splitters and Tanks close in, and every fifth wave the VOIDMAW boss arrives with radial bullet patterns and summoned minions. Kills drop XP shards; each level-up pauses the fight and offers a choice of three upgrade cards — damage, fire rate, multishot, pierce, crit, shields, magnet radius and more — so every run builds differently. Survive all 10 waves and slay the final VOIDMAW to win, then keep going in endless mode or chase a new best score. Synthesized WebAudio sound effects and a bass-and-arp music loop, particles, screen shake, floating damage numbers and a combo multiplier round out the fight. No network, no libraries — just canvas, ES modules and your reflexes.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in your browser.
